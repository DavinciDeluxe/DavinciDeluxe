## 🖥️ Two ways to tune

- **Desktop app (Windows)** — read, edit and write your ECU locally. Emissions & DTC-off are handled in the app with your license.
- **Cloud Tuning** — upload your original `.bin` from any browser and our automated engine returns a modified, checksum-corrected file, usually in minutes. No manual review, fully automated; if a service isn't available for your exact ECU the job is auto-rejected and your tokens refunded.

## ⚙️ System requirements

- **Windows 10 & 11** — both **32-bit and 64-bit** supported
- **Microsoft Edge** (WebView2) installed — ships with Windows 10/11 by default
- Internet connection (license activation + Cloud Tuning)
- Not available for macOS, Linux or mobile — Cloud Tuning works from any browser

## ❓ FAQ

**Is DaVinci Deluxe better than WinOLS?**
DaVinci Deluxe is built for speed and automation: automatic checksum correction, a huge DAMOS/ORI reference database and a fully-automated Cloud Tuning engine mean you spend less time hunting maps and correcting checksums by hand. WinOLS is a powerful manual map editor; DaVinci Deluxe focuses on getting a working, checksum-correct file out the door fast.

**Do you correct checksums automatically?**
Yes — checksum and CVN correction is automatic on both the desktop app and Cloud Tuning.

**Which ECUs do you support?**
Most cars and vans from ~2002 to 2026, 33+ brands, all major ECU families (Bosch EDC15/16/17, MD1/MG1, ME/MED, SIMOS/PCR/SID, Delphi, Marelli, Denso…). Check the full list: [Supported ECU list (PDF)](https://www.davincideluxe.fr/DaVinci_Deluxe_Supported_List.pdf).
